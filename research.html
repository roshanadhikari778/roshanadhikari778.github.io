Research & publications

Peer-reviewed research

Published work on the economics of irrigation and water resources, agricultural technology adoption, climate adaptation, and the political economy of development. My research examines how investments in agricultural water management, mechanisation, and risk management shape farm productivity, household welfare, and the distribution of economic opportunity across gender, caste, and class.
