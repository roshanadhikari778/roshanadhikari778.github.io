Evaluation & applied work

Selected assignments

Evaluation, impact assessment, and policy-research engagements for multilateral and bilateral organisations, UN agencies, and governments, applying OECD DAC and UNEG-aligned methods, contribution analysis, value-for-money assessment, and household surveys at scale.

2026–IFAD

M&E Framework Design & Oversight, Nine-Country Portfolio

International Consultant · Rome

Design and oversight of monitoring and evaluation frameworks for IFAD rural development and women's economic empowerment programmes across Niger, Nepal, Tunisia, Tanzania, Rwanda, Kiribati, Fiji, Solomon Islands, and Tonga, including outcome indicators and multi-country evaluation methodologies.

2026IFAD · FAO · UN Women · WFP

Annual & Mid-Term Review, JP RWEE Pacific

Lead Consultant · Fiji, Kiribati, Solomon Islands, Tonga

Independent OECD DAC and UNEG-aligned mixed-methods evaluation of the Joint Programme on Rural Women's Economic Empowerment; designed and implemented the midline household survey across the four countries, reporting against outcome indicators including savings group functionality, financial inclusion, and women's economic empowerment.

2025–26UN Women · FAO · IFAD · WFP

Annual Review & Mid-Term Review, JP RWEE Nepal

Lead Consultant · Madhesh Province

Mid-term contribution analysis of a four-agency UN consortium programme reaching 5,508 rural women across five municipalities: theory of change reconstruction, WEAI and GALS gender-transformative tools, GESI audits, and an annual monitoring survey using multi-stage cluster sampling.

2026World Bank

Institutional Review of Nepal's Public Sector Institutions

Economist · Washington, D.C.

Institutional capacity assessment of five federal bodies (Nepal Electricity Authority, Nepal Tourism Board, Department of Tourism, CIAA, National Information Commission), combining qualitative interviews, institutional analysis, and capacity-gap diagnosis.

2026International IDEA

Nepal Election Watch 2026

Quantitative Research Specialist · Nepal

Disaggregated statistical analysis of electoral inclusion (gender, caste and ethnicity, geography, disability, age) and systematic media content analysis for Nepal's 2026 federal parliamentary election.

2025–26Biratnagar Metropolitan City

Status of Women in Biratnagar Study

Economist · Nepal

Design and analysis of a 14,000+ household survey on women's socio-economic representation, economic participation, and experiences of violence, translated into municipal policy recommendations.

2025UNDP

Baseline Study, Green Transition Portfolio

Team Leader · Sudurpaschim Province, Nepal

Convergent parallel mixed-methods baseline establishing 38 indicators on green growth, climate resilience, and sustainable development, combining household surveys, an enterprise census, and multi-stakeholder consultations within OECD DAC framing.

End Evaluation of SSLI-5 & Proposal Development

Team Leader · Makwanpur, Nepal

OECD DAC end evaluation of a multi-sector integrated development project targeting 1,800 Chepang indigenous community members, with recommendations on scale-up and handover to local government.

2025British Council

Girls' Empowerment & Mainstreaming Sustainably (GEMS) Endline Evaluation

MEL Researcher · Nepal

Endline evaluation of a three-year adolescent girls' empowerment project across five districts (2,500 direct beneficiaries), applying gender-transformative evaluation methods and contribution analysis.
